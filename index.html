Our Philosophy

More than a market.
A community
rooted in the land.

The Grove was born from a simple belief: that the relationship between farmer and table should be personal, transparent, and joyful. We partner exclusively with producers who share our commitment to ecological stewardship and traditional craft.

Every vendor is vetted by our curatorial team. Every product tells a story. Come hungry, leave inspired.

The Grove Experience

A Saturday
ritual for the
discerning palate

More than shopping — The Grove is an immersive sensory experience. Taste before you buy, learn from the growers themselves, and discover ingredients you won't find anywhere else.

  • ✦
    Chef DemosLocal chefs cook live with market produce every Saturday at 10am and 12pm.
  • ✦
    Tasting StationsSample jams, cheeses, oils, and ferments directly from the makers.
  • ✦
    Children's GardenAn educational garden patch where young visitors learn to grow and harvest.
  • ✦
    Live MusicCurated acoustic performances set the tone for a leisurely morning.
